Coaching Notes

Summarize the main focus of the coaching. Include the agent's Primary Opportunity and Primary Limiter, then briefly explain what the performance data, call review, or coaching discussion showed.
  • Primary Opportunity — What metric or performance area needs improvement?
  • Primary Limiter — What is preventing the agent from improving?
  • Brief Explanation — What did you observe that supports this?
Show example
Primary Opportunity: TCR Primary Limiter: Leads Touched Brief Explanation: The agent's TCR is below target mainly because of low leads touched. We reviewed their activity and found incomplete contact attempts on Day 2–7 leads.

Action Plan

Document the agreed action plan and next steps for both the Agent and TL. Be specific about what each person will do, how often or by when, and how progress will be checked.
  • Agent Action — What specific behavior or action will the agent execute?
  • TL Action — What coaching, monitoring, or support will the TL provide?
  • Follow-Up — How and when will progress be checked?
Show example
Agent: Complete 2 sets of contact attempts for all eligible Day 2–7 leads daily and complete scheduled callbacks on time. TL: Review call cadence daily and provide real-time feedback on missed contact attempts. Follow-Up: Review TCR, leads touched, and cadence compliance during next week's coaching.
